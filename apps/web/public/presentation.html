02 The big picture

Field work in. Business clarity out.

Most contractors run their business across notebooks, phone calls and WhatsApp forwards. AzentisFieldOS replaces that with one connected system: what happens at the site is recorded at the site, and everything else follows from it.

Recorded at the site
Daily reports, material receipts and consumption, crew attendance, machinery use, expenses — entered on a phone as work happens.
Kept in order automatically
Stock balances, advance ledgers, payment records and site activity feeds update themselves from each entry.
Seen from the office
A live dashboard, site-wise activity, and site, inventory, labour, machinery and financial reports.

No re-typing, no reconciling registers at month end. One entry at the site becomes stock, ledger and report — in the same moment.

03 Who uses it

Built for two people who run your business.

No complicated permission matrix to administer. Two clear roles cover how a contracting firm actually works.

Owner / Admin

Runs the business

Sees everything, controls everything. The dashboard, every ledger and every report — plus the settings that shape the platform to the firm.

  • Company branding, users and roles
  • Material catalog, rates and low-stock thresholds
  • Report schedules and delivery preferences
Site Supervisor

Runs the site

Works from the phone at the site. Submits the daily report, receives materials, logs consumption, marks attendance — without ever touching company settings.

  • Daily site report with photos, from the field
  • Material receipt confirmation and consumption
  • Crew attendance and work records

05 Materials & inventory

Follow every bag of cement from purchase to slab.

Materials are where contractor money quietly leaks. The platform tracks each item through its full journey, and the stock position at the godown and at every site is always current.

Purchase
From a vendor, to the godown or straight to a site
Godown
Central stock, with low-stock alerts per material
Transfer
Godown to site, or site to site, tracked in transit
Receipt
The receiving site confirms the quantity that actually arrived
Consumption
Used in work, returned, or written off as wastage
Shortages surface immediately. Sent 50 bags, received 48? The gap is recorded at confirmation — not discovered at the end of the project.
Stock is never a mystery. The inventory screen shows godown and site-wise balances for every material and size, with low-stock items flagged for reorder.
06 A record you can trust

Entries are corrected, never quietly changed.

In this system, nothing that touches stock or money can be edited or deleted. A mistake is fixed by a correction entry that says who, what and why — so the history always tells the truth.

Every entry has an author

Each record carries who made it and when. Accountability is built into the data, not enforced by follow-up calls.

11 One connected day

Watch a single day move through the system.

8:30 am · Godown

Cement leaves for Sunrise Tower

A transfer of 50 bags is recorded. Godown stock drops, the consignment shows as in transit.

10:15 am · Site

The supervisor confirms receipt — 48 bags

Two bags short. The shortage is captured at the gate, the site's stock rises by exactly what arrived.

2:00 pm · Site

An advance is given at the site

₹5,000 to a mason, entered in a minute. His outstanding balance is current before the office hears about it.

6:30 pm · Site

The daily report goes in — photos and all

Work done, 24 crew, 42 bags consumed, 12 m³ of M25, the day's expenses. Weak signal? It syncs when the phone finds network.

6:31 pm · Office

The owner already knows

The dashboard shows the day across all five sites — and flags the one that stayed silent. No calls made, no registers opened.
