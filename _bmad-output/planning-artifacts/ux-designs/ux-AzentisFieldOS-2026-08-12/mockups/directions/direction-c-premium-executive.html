C — Premium Executive Dashboard

This direction treats the Owner/Admin's screen as a command instrument, not a spreadsheet: warm-ink "premium paper" surfaces, real tabular numerals, sparklines and small multiples in place of raw tables, and a strict two-accent discipline — deep teal for action, gold reserved only for money — so financial figures read at a glance across every screen. Elevation comes from soft, precise shadows rather than blur, giving the Owner the feeling of piloting the business rather than scrolling through it. The Site Supervisor's mobile flow deliberately drops almost all of this vocabulary in favour of one big teal button and plain state — because the executive posture is for the person reading the business, not the person reporting from the field.
