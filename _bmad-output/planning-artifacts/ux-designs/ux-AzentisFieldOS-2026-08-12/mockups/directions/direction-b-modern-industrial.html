AzentisFieldOS — Visual Direction Comparison

B — Modern Industrial / Construction

A confident, structural register built for people who work with quantities and numbers all day: bold grid discipline, sharp geometric corners, heavier type weights on data and labels, and an earthy concrete-and-graphite palette. A single rust/amber signal color is reserved strictly for alerts and flags — never for decoration — so attention states carry real weight instead of competing with wallpaper. Premium reads through restraint and precision, not through a construction-site costume.
