Direction A of 3 · Comparative UX Design

A — Premium Minimal SaaS

A restrained, editorial system built on hairline borders, a near-monochrome warm-neutral palette, and one deliberate accent — Kiln Ochre — spent only where it earns attention. No gradients, no card shadows, no decorative icons; the craft comes from spacing, tabular numbers, and typographic hierarchy, the way Linear or Stripe would approach it.

It suits AzentisFieldOS because the product's real subject is trust and precision with money and materials moving through a site — a quiet, confident interface signals control and reliability rather than trying to entertain a busy contractor.
