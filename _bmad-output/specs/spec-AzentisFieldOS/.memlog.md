- (capability) CAP-1 Multi-Site Management. intent: Owner/Admin creates and manages multiple Sites, with both an individual Site view and a consolidated cross-Site rollup. success: a new Site appears immediately in both views with no separate config step; every record tagged to a Site is retrievable from that Site's view. (PRD FR-1..FR-3)
- (capability) CAP-2 Material Catalog Configuration. intent: Owner/Admin defines and extends Material categories, Materials, Sizes/Specs, Units, and Custom Fields without a code change. success: a newly added Material/Size/Unit is immediately selectable in Purchase/Movement/Consumption forms; disabling a Material hides it from new entries while historical records stay intact. (PRD FR-4..FR-7)
- (capability) CAP-3 Inventory Lifecycle & Movement. intent: track every unit of Material through Purchase -> Godown Stock -> Site Transfer -> Site Stock -> Consumption -> Return/Wastage, across Vendor->Godown, Godown->Site, Vendor->Site direct, and Site->Site. success: current Stock is always derivable by replaying transaction history; a transaction driving Stock negative is rejected, not allowed. (PRD FR-8..FR-14)
- (capability) CAP-4 Machinery & Vehicle Registers. intent: track Machinery/Vehicles by current Site with fuel/maintenance/repair logging and movement history. success: current Site updates the moment a movement is recorded, visible from both the asset's record and the Site view. (PRD FR-15..FR-18)
- (capability) CAP-5 Labour & Team Management. intent: one tenant-wide Team Member pool, never permanently assigned to a Site; daily Work Records capture who actually worked where. success: a Team Member can work different Sites on different dates freely, but never two Sites on the same date. (PRD FR-19..FR-21)
- (capability) CAP-6 Labour Advances & Payments. intent: Owner/Admin records Advances and Payments with fully manual, owner-controlled Advance Adjustments, never automatic deduction. success: Outstanding Balance changes only via explicit Adjustment, capped so it can never go negative; full adjustment history retained. (PRD FR-22..FR-25)
- (capability) CAP-7 RMC Tracking. intent: record RMC delivered/used per Site, Vendor, grade, and cost. success: Site-wise and Vendor-wise RMC totals reconcile exactly to the sum of individual entries in range. (PRD FR-26..FR-27)
- (capability) CAP-8 Daily Site Report (DSR). intent: Site Supervisor completes one DSR per Site per day from a phone (work, labour, materials, RMC, machinery/vehicles, expenses, issues, photos), fully offline-capable. success: a DSR completed offline is never lost and syncs automatically on reconnect, with per-sub-record idempotency preventing duplicates. (PRD FR-28..FR-31)
- (capability) CAP-9 Automated Report Generation & Delivery. intent: compile a branded daily report per Site from that day's DSR and deliver it to the Owner automatically (WhatsApp/Email/in-app), no manual send. success: a submitted DSR results in same-day delivery without user action; failed delivery retries then surfaces in-app rather than silently dropping. (PRD FR-32..FR-33)
- (capability) CAP-10 Contractor Dashboard. intent: one dashboard rolling up Projects, Today's Activity, Inventory, Team, Machinery/Vehicles, with drill-down. success: every summary figure reconciles exactly to underlying records; a Site with no DSR today is visibly flagged. (PRD FR-34..FR-38)
- (capability) CAP-11 Vendor Management. intent: maintain a Vendor database with full Purchase->Material->Quantity->Amount history per Vendor. success: a Vendor's displayed total matches the exact sum of their individual Purchase records. (PRD FR-39..FR-40)
- (capability) CAP-12 Expense Tracking. intent: record Site-linked Expenses against admin-configurable categories. success: every Expense is retrievable by Site/category/date range; category list is never hardcoded. (PRD FR-41)
- (capability) CAP-13 Reports. intent: a defined set of Site/Inventory/Labour/Machinery-Vehicle/Financial reports, each strictly Tenant-scoped. success: no report, under any filter, can surface another Tenant's data. (PRD FR-42..FR-46)
- (capability) CAP-14 Admin Configuration. intent: Owner/Admin configures branding, users/roles, sites, inventory, labour, machinery/vehicle types, expense/vendor categories, report templates, notification channels, without a code deployment. success: a branding change reflects in the next generated report with no separate publish step. (PRD FR-47..FR-51)
- (capability) CAP-15 Multi-Tenant / White-Label Platform. intent: each Tenant runs as a fully isolated, independently branded instance provisioned from one shared codebase. success: a newly provisioned Tenant has zero visibility into any other Tenant's data, verified by absence of any network path between deployments, not just an app-layer check. (PRD FR-52..FR-53, reconciled to deploy-per-tenant architecture)
- (capability) CAP-16 Audit & Transaction History. intent: Material, Advance, Machinery/Vehicle, and Payment transactions are never overwritten or deleted; corrections are new, linked, reason-carrying entries. success: no UI/API path can update or delete a past transaction; a correction without a reason is rejected. (PRD FR-54)
- (constraint) Tenant isolation is absolute: no cross-tenant data path may ever exist, enforced by deployment separation (architecture companion AD-1), not by an in-app check alone.
- (constraint) Advances, Stock, and Payments are never auto-adjusted or auto-deducted; every change to them requires explicit, reason-carrying user action, logged append-only (CAP-16).
- (constraint) No approval-chain, hierarchy, or mandatory workflow may be introduced anywhere in the product — this is a simple owner-operated tool, not an enterprise ERP.
- (constraint) Every material type, size, unit, labour category, machinery/vehicle type, and expense category must be admin-configurable; none may be hardcoded.
- (constraint) DSR entry must function fully offline on a low-end phone over a 2G/3G-equivalent connection; the network is an optimization, not a dependency (CAP-8).
- (constraint) Platform is responsive web only for v1 — no native mobile app.
- (constraint) WCAG AA accessibility and Lighthouse >95 (Performance/Accessibility/Best Practices/SEO) are enforced in CI, not left to discretionary review.
- (constraint) The pilot contractor's payment is contingent on delivered product quality, not a fixed spec-and-price contract — completeness and correctness of this contract carry direct commercial weight.
- (constraint) Infrastructure cost is linear per Tenant under the deploy-per-tenant architecture; this must inform any future pricing model rather than being decided independently of it.
- (nongoal) No native mobile app in v1.
- (nongoal) No formal billing, invoicing, BOQ management, or GST/invoicing workflow.
- (nongoal) No purchase-order workflow, project costing/budgeting module, or approval chains anywhere.
- (nongoal) No GPS-based tracking for vehicles, machinery, or attendance.
- (nongoal) No formal equipment-maintenance-management workflow beyond basic logging (CAP-4).
- (nongoal) No document/drawing management system beyond attachments already on DSR/Purchase/RMC records.
- (nongoal) No client portal for the government/private client side of a contract.
- (nongoal) No WhatsApp chatbot — WhatsApp is one-way automated report delivery only (CAP-9).
- (nongoal) No AI-generated summaries, cost/progress forecasting, or predictive analysis.
- (nongoal) No accounting/GST software integration.
- (nongoal) No in-app cross-tenant admin surface of any kind — Tenant provisioning is a credential-level, off-app concern (architecture AD-11).
